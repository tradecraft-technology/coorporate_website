# Hero artwork

Created for TradeCraft with the built-in image generation tool on September 8, 2026. The production assets are `workshop-scene.webp` and `clouds.webp`; their alpha channels must be preserved. `social-preview.jpg` is a browser screenshot of the implemented hero.

## Landscape — final generation prompt

A TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND PNG ASSET. Wide landscape aspect ratio 16:9, 1672 by 941 preferred. This is a game-art foreground cutout with real RGBA transparency, NOT a full illustration with sky. Paint a detailed beautiful cinematic painterly landscape seen from the open front of a timber craftsman's workshop in North Texas. ALL SKY MUST BE TRANSPARENT: at least the entire top-left 50% area empty transparent, as well as holes in the leaves. Absolutely no blue sky, white sky, colored background, checkerboard, typography or UI. Only objects with actual transparent background. Composition: lower-right 45% contains a beautifully worn honey-brown wooden workbench angled in perspective. On the bench: rolled architectural plans, steel carpenter's square, yellow and black tape measure, work gloves, and one angled dark tablet showing a fine ivory architectural roof diagram. The bench occupies x=35%-100%, y=73%-100%. Right edge is a vertical timber workshop post. A large canopy of sunlit golden-green live oak leaves hangs from the upper-right and top edge; canopy covers x=45%-100%, y=0%-27%, with natural leaf edges and lots of tiny transparent gaps. Behind workbench middle-right is a pale warm limestone house with steep roof, a neatly placed ladder and stacks of shingles (house x=64%-90%, y=36%-75%). Across lower-left and middle, beautiful sunny neighborhood with pale brick/limestone houses, sloping roofs, mature green trees, curving lane, bluebonnet wildflowers in bottom left. Gently rolling wooded hills on horizon at y=55%, rising to right. No human figures. Golden angled morning sunlight from upper right, richly detailed hand painted animated-film background aesthetic with painterly tactile brushwork, warm greens, honey sunlight, cream stone houses. Atmosphere of a beautiful good day's work, grounded craftsmanship and quiet useful technology. The transparency is CRITICAL: output actual alpha=0 pixels everywhere above the horizon between objects, top-left area completely empty alpha, like cutout foreground layers in a 2D game. Keep shapes crisp and detailed with softened alpha edges and no gray/white color fringe. Transparent background, not checkerboard illustration.

## Clouds — final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: transparent cloud-only layer for a sunlit painterly landscape website, wide horizontal 3:1 aspect ratio. Paint one beautiful loosely connected bank of delicate small fair-weather clouds, elongated across the middle of the canvas. Artistically painted wispy edges and broken wisps, soft sunlit ivory highlights with very subtle cool pale blue grey undersides, lightly textured brushwork like a sophisticated hand-painted animated film background. These are light airy cirrocumulus and small soft fair weather clouds, NOT heavy towering storm clouds or gray smoke, not dense cotton balls. Cloud bank fills about middle 45% of canvas height, with large transparent padding above and below. Left and right edges fade to entirely transparent, no elements cropped at canvas edges. The entire background is genuinely TRANSPARENT ALPHA, no sky color, no ground, no scene, no text, no borders, no checkerboard pixels. Slight semi-transparency in fine edges. White cloud highlights should retain their lightness when composited over blue sky. Original beautiful painterly clouds only. Will slowly drift horizontally behind trees; keep diffuse delicate feel rather than sharp photoreal photographic cutout.
